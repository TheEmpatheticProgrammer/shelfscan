# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name** (30 chars max)
Shelfsight: Bookshelf Scanner

**Short description** (80 chars max)
Photograph your shelves to catalog your books, then see what to read next.

**Full description** (4,000 chars max)

Turn the books on your shelves into a library you can search, sort and carry with you.

**Scan your shelves**
Take a photo of a row of spines or a stack of covers, and Shelfsight reads every title it can see. Check the list,
fix anything misread, and add them in one go. For a single book, scan its barcode or type its title.

**A library that looks like one**
Large covers, grouped by author or genre. Mark books as unread, reading or read, keep the ones you're reading at the
top, and sort by title, author or when you added them.

**Know each book**
Every book gets a short, spoiler-free synopsis and a row of similar books.

**What to read next**
Suggestions from what you already own, focused on any author or genre you choose, with a line on why each one fits.
More by your favourite authors, upcoming releases from the authors on your shelf, and a Want to read list for the ones
you don't own yet.

**Yours to keep**
Your library lives on your phone. Export it to a file at any time and import it on a new phone. No account, no ads.
